# ChinaTap Screen Translator — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

ChinaTap Screen Translator ("the app") translates Chinese text in screenshots and images into English or Russian. It is built to work on your iPhone without sending your content anywhere.

## What the app processes

- **Screenshots and images** you send to the app (through a Shortcut, the Action Button, or by choosing an image in the app).
- **Text recognized** in those images, and the **translations** the app produces.

All of this is processed **only on your iPhone**. Text recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Translation uses an AI model that runs locally on the device.

## What the app does not do

- It does **not** upload your screenshots, recognized text or translations to any server.
- It does **not** use accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
- It does **not** collect personal data, device identifiers or location.
- It does **not** sell or share any data, because it collects none.

## Model download

After you tap **Download AI Model**, iOS downloads the translation model (about 1.1 GB) from Apple's App Store servers using Apple's Background Assets service. This download is handled by Apple, just like downloading the app itself, and contains no screenshot or text data. You can also import the model file yourself from the Files app.

## Data stored on your device

- Your settings (target language, how results are shown).
- The most recent translation, so you can reopen it while the app is running.
- The downloaded AI model.
- Small numeric diagnostics (timings and counts, no images or text) kept in the app's cache on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere and are limited in number.

Deleting the app removes all of this data.

## Photos

The app asks for permission to **add** images to your photo library only when you tap **Save**. It never reads your photo library in the background; when you choose an image yourself, iOS passes only that image to the app.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
